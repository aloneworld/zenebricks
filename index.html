ABOUT THE BRAND
Our story / Since 2021

Creativity Without Boundaries

ZENE is a company creating compatible LEGO brick sets, founded in 2021 and driven by a deep passion for creativity and innovation.

Our name draws inspiration from Hungarian culture, where “ZENE” represents artistry and creativity. It also reflects the infinite reflection and contemplation found in Zen Buddhism—signifying our commitment to limitless imagination.

The world of LEGO has no boundaries, and every story holds infinite possibilities. We celebrate the creativity, uniqueness and problem-solving spirit within every creation while staying true to the core brick-building experience.
